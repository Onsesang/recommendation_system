# Phase 1 — Configurable Tactile Taxonomy

Taxonomy version: `tactile_axes_v1`. Candidate axes are declarative; the active subset is selected only by Phase 3 diagnostics.

| Axis | Negative pole | Positive pole | Definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| softness | soft | firm | Perceived yielding softness versus resistant firmness of the textile or garment material. |
| surface_texture | smooth | rough | Smooth versus rough, scratchy, or coarse character of the textile surface. |
| elasticity | non_elastic | elastic | Resistance to stretch versus ability to stretch and recover as a material property. |
| thickness | thin | thick | Low versus high perceived textile thickness, including explicit thinness or thickness. |
| flexibility | flexible | stiff | Ease of bending and draping versus stiffness or rigidity of the textile. |
| warmth | warm | cool | Perceived thermal warmth versus coolness attributable to the textile. |
| sponginess | spongy | crisp | Compressible spongy hand versus crisp, sharply structured textile hand. |
